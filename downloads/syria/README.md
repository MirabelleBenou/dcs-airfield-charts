# Syria — all airfields and heliports

One PDF per airfield and helicopter base (all its pages: the vicinity page 10-8, the 10-9, the parking pages 10-9A, 10-9B… when needed, and the instrument approach pages, 11-1, 11-2… for aircraft and 11-1H, 11-2H… for helicopters, for each runway direction with a beacon). Magnetic declination for **1985**. Every page as PNG (kneeboard) and SVG (Inkscape layers) is in the two zips attached to the latest release: [airfields](https://github.com/MirabelleBenou/dcs-airfield-charts/releases/latest/download/DCS_Airfield_Charts_Syria_1985_airfields.zip) and [helicopters](https://github.com/MirabelleBenou/dcs-airfield-charts/releases/latest/download/DCS_Airfield_Charts_Syria_1985_helicopters.zip).

**Heliport directory**: the 141 other heliports of Syria, four to a page with a plan of their pads and pad numbers, position, elevation and nearest town, and an index: [Syria_heliports_directory.pdf](Syria_heliports_directory.pdf?raw=true) (the pages as JPEG for the kneeboard are in the helicopters zip, folder `heliports/`).
