# Cold War Germany — airfields

One PDF per airfield (all its pages: the vicinity page 10-8, the 10-9, the parking pages 10-9A, 10-9B… when needed, and the instrument approach pages, 11-1, 11-2… for aircraft and 11-1H, 11-2H… for helicopters, for each runway direction with a beacon). Magnetic declination for **1985**. Every page as PNG (kneeboard) and SVG (Inkscape layers) is in the [airfields zip](https://github.com/MirabelleBenou/dcs-airfield-charts/releases/latest/download/DCS_Airfield_Charts_GermanyColdWar_1985_airfields.zip) attached to the latest release.

The charts carry the DCS name of the airfield, with its ICAO code (76 airfields) and the taxiway names from OpenStreetMap where it has them (data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). The 24 grass strips, whose runway the F10 map does not draw, are charted from the runway DCS declares; Landstuhl, whose declared runway is 10 m long, is charted as a helicopter base (in the helicopters zip).

**Heliport directory**: the 108 heliports of Cold War Germany, four to a page with a plan of their pads and pad numbers, position, elevation and nearest town, and an index: [GermanyColdWar_heliports_directory.pdf](GermanyColdWar_heliports_directory.pdf?raw=true) (the pages as JPEG for the kneeboard are in the [helicopters zip](https://github.com/MirabelleBenou/dcs-airfield-charts/releases/latest/download/DCS_Airfield_Charts_GermanyColdWar_1985_helicopters.zip)).
